Dataset Notes and Annotations

During the dataset uploading process, a small number of images were unintentionally lost and are no longer available. The missing samples are distributed as follows:

Fog: 6 images

Rain: 9 images

Snow: 4 images

These missing images do not affect the validity of the experimental results reported in the paper. To verify this, we re-ran the experiments after removing the corresponding annotations, and the performance differences were found to be negligible.

We recommend that future users remove the invalid annotations associated with these missing images when using the dataset.

Class Definitions

The annotation labels follow the format below:

# Classes
names:
  0: mandatory
  1: prohibitory
  2: warning


mandatory: Mandatory traffic signs

prohibitory: Prohibitory traffic signs

warning: Warning traffic signs
